FAQ

Fair questions.

Which platforms do you support?

GA4, Google Ads, Meta Ads and Search Console at launch. We'd rather run four connectors flawlessly than forty flakily — more come when these are bulletproof.

Is my clients' data safe?

Read-only access, granted by the account owner through Google and Meta's own consent screens, revocable anytime. We never sell or share data. See our privacy policy.

Isn't Looker Studio free?

Yes — and every agency using it pays in hours instead: templates that break with every GA4 change, and no narrative your client can actually follow. Free tools cost your weekends.

Can I edit the AI summary?

Always. It's a draft that's 95% done, not a bot speaking on your behalf.
