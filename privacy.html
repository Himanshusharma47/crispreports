Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

CrispReports ("we", "us") provides white-label marketing reporting for agencies and freelance marketers. This policy explains what data we handle and how. The short version: we read marketing data you explicitly connect, we use it only to generate your reports, and we never sell it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, agency name, and branding assets (logo, colors) you upload.
  • Connected platform data: when you (or your client) connect Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, Google Search Console, or Meta Ads via their official consent screens, we receive read-only access tokens and retrieve the metrics needed to build reports (e.g. sessions, conversions, ad spend, impressions, clicks).
  • Report recipients: email addresses you designate to receive reports.
  • Waitlist submissions: email and the answers you provide on the signup form.

How we use it

  • To generate and deliver the monthly reports you configure — including producing a written summary of the connected data using AI models.
  • To operate, support, and improve the service.
  • To email you about your account and, for waitlist members, about launch. Unsubscribe anytime.

Google API Services — Limited Use disclosure

CrispReports' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the reporting features you request; we do not transfer it to third parties except as necessary to provide those features, comply with law, or as part of a merger/acquisition with prior notice; we do not use it for advertising; and we do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security purposes, or to comply with law.

AI processing

Report summaries are generated by sending aggregated metrics (never your clients' personal data) to our AI provider under terms that prohibit training on your data.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data or your clients' data.
  • We never request write access to your marketing accounts.
  • We never contact your clients except to deliver the reports you schedule.

Service providers

We use a small set of infrastructure subprocessors (hosting, database, email delivery, AI generation, payments). Each receives only what it needs to perform its function.

Data retention & deletion

Connected-platform tokens are stored encrypted and can be revoked by you at any time — from CrispReports, or directly from your Google or Meta security settings. Delete your account and we delete your data, including stored tokens and generated reports, within 30 days. You can also request deletion by email.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access tokens are stored encrypted and are never exposed to other users or third parties.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: hello@crispreports.com.

We'll update this policy as the product evolves and note material changes here.
